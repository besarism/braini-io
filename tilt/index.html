Tilt
A proj0 project

Your desktop folds with the lid.

Tilt reads the MacBook hinge and bends your live desktop in real time. Close the lid and the screen tilts away, softens and shades; open it and everything settles back. Rendered with Metal, nothing recorded, nothing uploaded.

macOS 14 or later Apple silicon MacBook with a lid sensor Screen Recording permission
Looping demo. The real thing follows your hinge.
